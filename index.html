OUR SCIENCE

Understanding how the liver responds to disease and injury.

The YAN LAB investigates the molecular and cellular mechanisms underlying liver injury and hepatotoxicity.

Our research integrates basic and translational approaches to understand how metabolic, toxic, and disease-associated stresses disrupt hepatic homeostasis and contribute to liver disease.

A CENTRAL QUESTION

Why does liver injury occur — and what determines its outcome?

The liver possesses remarkable mechanisms for maintaining homeostasis and repairing injury. However, persistent or excessive stress can overwhelm these protective mechanisms and contribute to progressive disease.

We seek to understand the molecular, cellular, and tissue-level processes that determine whether the liver adapts, repairs itself, or progresses toward chronic injury.
