PRINCIPAL INVESTIGATOR

Shengmin Yan, Ph.D.

Assistant Professor

Department of Pathology and Laboratory Medicine
Tulane University School of Medicine

Dr. Shengmin Yan's research focuses on mechanisms of liver injury caused by liver diseases and hepatotoxicity.

His research program includes alcohol-associated liver disease, alcohol-associated hepatitis, environmental hepatotoxicity, metabolic liver disease, cholestatic liver injury, autophagy, protein condensates, and the hepatic microenvironment.
